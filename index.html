Research

DNN robustness benchmarking

Perceptron Benchmark is a toolbox for measuring the safety and security of deep neural networks. It estimates a model's true robustness from both sides: tightening the empirical upper bound with state-of-the-art attacks, and raising the theoretical lower bound with linear-approximation-based verification. It is framework-agnostic and supports classification and detection models on PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras and cloud APIs.

codedocs

Adversarial machine learning in the physical world

Attacks on self-driving and ML-as-a-service pipelines: physical adversarial patches that fool object detectors, fingerprinting attacks against commercial MLaaS APIs, adversarial examples that transfer across real-world vision tasks, and the first adversarial attack against multiple object tracking.

papers

Structure-aware fuzzing

Bit-flipping fuzzers such as AFL and libFuzzer struggle when inputs are large and highly structured. Libprotobuf-mutator for Apollo learns the input format from protocol buffers and generates spec-conforming inputs, reaching deep program logic in the Baidu Apollo autonomous-driving platform. It is now integrated upstream.

Autonomous vehicle application security

OpenCAV / AVGuard. As U-M opened its autonomous vehicle to third-party developers, we built static and dynamic vetting for self-driving apps that may be flawed, vulnerable or malicious. TrafficNet is a large-scale, extensible library of naturalistic driving scenarios that turns research datasets into something vehicle engineers can actually use.

Mobile & IoT security

Open ports in Android apps. A static analysis tool that found 400+ apps with exploitable open ports, enabling remote theft of contacts and photos, malware installation and code execution — including session hijacking of AirDroid (CVE-2016-5227).

Covered by WIRED, TechRepublic, Mashable, Bleeping Computer, Axios, Digital Trends and others.

ContexIoT. App patching that brings contextual integrity to commodity smart-home apps with minimal overhead. SInspector. Exposes misuse of Unix domain sockets in popular Android apps and system daemons, including a privilege escalation in KingRoot.
